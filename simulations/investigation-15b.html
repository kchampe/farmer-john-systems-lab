Investigation 15B — Adding More Greenhouse Gases

Begin with Earth already in its natural greenhouse equilibrium.

NATURAL GREENHOUSE EQUILIBRIUM — BALANCED
Start: Earth already has an atmosphere and a natural greenhouse effect. Energy entering from the Sun is balanced by energy leaving to space.
Energy imbalance: At the old temperature, the Earth system is less effective at losing energy to space. Energy accumulates and the system begins to warm.
New equilibrium: As the Earth system warms, it emits more thermal radiation. Eventually outgoing energy once again balances incoming energy, but the system now contains more stored energy and is warmer than before.
