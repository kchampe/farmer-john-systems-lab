Farmer John’s Fertilizer Tanks

Investigation 9 — Same Equilibrium, Different Residence Time
Farmer John wants to add a liquid fertilizer to his irrigation water. Both tanks below contain the same amount of water and both are in dynamic equilibrium, but water moves through them at different rates. Add the same fertilizer dose to both tanks and compare how long it remains in each system.

Tank A — Slow Turnover

Dynamic equilibriumYES
Water stored100 units
Fertilizer concentration0.0%
Calculated residence time50 s

Tank B — Fast Turnover

Dynamic equilibriumYES
Water stored100 units
Fertilizer concentration0.0%
Calculated residence time10 s
Both tanks begin in dynamic equilibrium.
Elapsed time after fertilizer addition: 0.0 s
Residence time = amount stored ÷ rate leaving
Tank A: 100 ÷ 2 = 50 s     |     Tank B: 100 ÷ 10 = 10 s
Residence time is an average timescale. In a well-mixed tank, fertilizer concentration declines gradually rather than every molecule leaving at exactly the residence time.
