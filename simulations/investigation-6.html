Farmer John's Emergency Siphon

Investigation 6 prototype — threshold behavior

After the float-valve incident, Farmer John decides that his water tank needs a simpler emergency backup for very wet weather.

He installs a siphon with its intake extending down into the tank. The pipe passes through the tank wall at a higher level. Under ordinary conditions, no water leaves through the siphon. If the water rises high enough to fill the bend in the pipe, the siphon can start and rapidly drain water from the tank.

John is ready to simulate a heavy-rain event and see when the system changes.

Starting condition: Water IN = 5 units/s and Normal OUT = 5 units/s. The tank begins in dynamic equilibrium.
