Investigation 16 — Ice, Albedo, and Positive Feedback

The previous animations used a simplified Earth. This model adds a new part of the Earth system: snow and ice. Take a moment to familiarize yourself with the new setup.

START — NATURAL GREENHOUSE EQUILIBRIUM WITHOUT ICE IN THIS SIMPLIFIED MODEL
New system component: Snow and ice have high albedo. They reflect a larger fraction of incoming sunlight back to space, so less solar energy is absorbed by the Earth system.
Part 1 — Adding ice: Reflection increases, absorption decreases, and stored energy falls until the system reaches a cooler equilibrium.
Part 2 — Ice–albedo positive feedback: Additional greenhouse gases initiate warming. Warming melts snow and ice → the darker surface reflects less sunlight → more solar energy is absorbed → additional warming causes still more melting.
Amplified response: The system eventually balances again as the warmer Earth emits more thermal energy to space. Greenhouse gases initiated the warming; the ice–albedo feedback increased the total response.
