Farmer John's Water Tank

A feedback experiment

Farmer John has a water-storage tank. Water continuously enters and leaves the tank, so its water level normally remains about the same. The tank is in dynamic equilibrium.

While repairing a toilet, John notices its float valve. As the toilet tank fills, the float rises and gradually reduces the incoming water. John decides to install the same kind of system on the inlet to his water tank. If rain adds extra water, he expects the rising float to reduce Water IN and help return the tank toward its usual level.

John likes doing his own repairs, although things do not always work correctly on his first attempt. He has finished installing the float and is ready to test it.

Starting conditions: Water IN = 5 units/s and Water OUT = 5 units/s. The tank begins in dynamic equilibrium.
