Investigation 15A — Building Earth’s Natural Greenhouse Effect

Start with no atmosphere, then add an atmosphere with greenhouse gases.

NO ATMOSPHERE — BALANCED, LOWER STORED ENERGY
Start: Without an atmosphere, solar energy enters, some is reflected, and absorbed energy is quickly reradiated to space. The system is in dynamic equilibrium with relatively little stored energy.
New equilibrium: Greenhouse gases absorb and emit infrared radiation. At first, outgoing energy decreases and energy accumulates within the Earth–atmosphere system. As the system warms, outgoing energy increases until it once again balances incoming energy. The resulting warmer equilibrium is Earth’s natural greenhouse effect.
