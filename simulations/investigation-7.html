When a System Doesn't Return

Farmer John's Tank — Investigation 7 prototype

Farmer John has decided that his tank operates best when it is about 50% full. At that level, Water IN and Normal OUT are both 5 units/s, so the tank remains in dynamic equilibrium.

Now John wants to run a rainstorm test. In the simulation, the rainstorm is represented by temporarily increasing Water IN. The storm will end automatically, but the siphon may continue operating after the rain is gone.

Starting condition: The tank begins empty. Turn on the water and establish Farmer John’s preferred 50% equilibrium before running the rainstorm.
This test simulates a temporary rainstorm by increasing Water IN from 5.0 to 8.0 units/s. The rain continues briefly after the siphon activates, then stops automatically and Water IN returns to 5.0.
