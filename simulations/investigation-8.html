Investigation 8 Prototype — Pond Liner Feedback V7.13

Farmer John has a lined irrigation pond. He planted trees to reduce sunlight and wind across the pond, which lowered evaporation enough that he could reduce the constant refill rate. The system worked well during mild summer conditions. Now he wants to know what happens during an unusually hot period.

Farmer John's Test

The test temporarily increases summer heat. The hot period ends automatically and returns to the original heat level. Watch whether the pond also returns to its original state.

What is the system doing?

The pond begins in dynamic equilibrium. The trees reduce evaporation enough that the reduced constant refill keeps up with water loss.

What to watch

As the pond level falls, more of the dark liner on the sloping sides becomes exposed to sunlight. The liner does not suddenly “switch on.” Its effect grows gradually as more dark surface is exposed.
